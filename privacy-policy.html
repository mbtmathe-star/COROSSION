1. Introduction

Corrosion Mitigation Services ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to this website and individuals who contact us. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the rights available to you under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and applicable Namibian data protection law.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through this website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, country, industry, and the details of your message. We may also collect standard technical information (such as browser type and IP address) automatically through normal website operation.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, provide quotes and technical recommendations, and to communicate with you about products and services relevant to your request. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Legal Basis For Processing

We process personal information on the basis of your consent when you submit a form, and/or our legitimate business interest in responding to commercial enquiries, consistent with the requirements of POPIA.

5. Sharing Of Information

We may share relevant enquiry details with our manufacturing principal (CSNRI / Critica Infrastructure) where necessary to provide technical support, product specification, or warranty-related assistance for a specific enquiry.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, or unauthorised access.

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

8. Cookies

This website may use essential cookies required for basic functionality. A full cookie policy will be published here once the site's analytics and marketing tooling is finalised.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.
