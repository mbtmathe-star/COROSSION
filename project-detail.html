Warehouse Steel Frame

Home/Projects/Warehouse Steel Frame

The Challenge

A regional distributor needed a structural steel frame for a new 40,000 sq ft warehouse extension, on a tight timeline before their peak shipping season.

Our Solution

Our crew handled fabrication and on-site structural welding as a single scope, running two welding teams in parallel to hold the schedule without cutting corners on inspection.

  • Full structural steel frame, certified MIG welding
  • Load-bearing columns and roof support trusses
  • In-process and final visual weld inspection
  • Coordinated scheduling around the client's operations
"ArcForge handled our warehouse steel frame ahead of schedule and every weld passed inspection first time." — Michael Anderson, Operations Director

The Result

The frame was completed nine days ahead of schedule, passing every structural inspection on the first pass and clearing the way for the client's peak-season build-out.
