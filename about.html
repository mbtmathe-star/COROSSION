Who We Are

The Regional Partner Behind Proven Composite Technology

Corrosion Mitigation Services is the authorized distributor of CSNRI composite pipe repair and corrosion-protection systems across South Africa and Namibia. CSNRI is part of Critica Infrastructure, a Henkel company, whose composite repair technology has been engineered, tested and deployed on critical infrastructure in more than 75 countries.

We exist to bring that same engineering-backed technology, product support and technical training to operators, contractors and asset owners across our region — without operators needing to look overseas for a proven repair solution.

We are an independent regional distributor and are not a subsidiary of Henkel or Critica Infrastructure. Our role is to supply, support and locally service the composite systems our principal manufactures.
