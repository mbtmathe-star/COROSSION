Common Questions

Frequently
Asked Questions

Answers to what clients ask us most. If you don't see what you need, reach out directly and we'll get back to you quickly.

Do you offer emergency welding repairs?+

Yes — we run a 24/7 emergency response line. Our mobile welding units can typically be on-site within a few hours for equipment breakdowns and urgent structural repairs.

What materials can you weld?+

Carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminum and most common alloys, using MIG, TIG or stick welding depending on the material, thickness and finish requirements.

Do you provide on-site welding services?+

Yes, our mobile units are fully equipped for on-site repairs, installs and fabrication work — no need to transport equipment to our shop for most jobs.

Are your welders certified?+

Every welder on our floor holds current AWS certification (D1.1 structural, with several also holding D1.6 stainless and pipe certifications), renewed on schedule.

How do you price a job?+

Common repairs use flat call-out pricing. Fabrication and structural work gets a written itemized quote after an on-site or drawing-based assessment — no surprise costs.

Do you carry insurance?+

Yes, ArcForge is fully licensed and carries general liability and workers' compensation coverage. Certificates are available on request for site work.

What's your typical turnaround time?+

Small repairs are often same-day. Fabrication projects depend on scope — we'll give you a realistic timeline as part of your quote.

Do you work outside Pittsburgh?+

We primarily serve the greater Pittsburgh region but take on larger fabrication projects further out — get in touch and we'll confirm coverage for your location.
